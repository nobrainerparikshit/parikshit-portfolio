EcoCredit

Making recycling rewarding through an IoT-enabled credit system.

October 2024 — October 2025 · IoT · Recycling · Behavioural design

About the project

EcoCredit rewards recycling with virtual credits, exploring how incentives can increase participation in sustainable waste management. The robotic recycling system connects a physical recycling action to a digital reward.

The project won first place at KIIT IDEATHON 2.0 in June 2025 among more than 300 participants.
