Abstract

I’m Parikshit, a student from India working across robotics, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and sustainable engineering. I build systems that connect research to real problems: robots for water restoration, voice-based health research, and recycling incentives. My current work spans computational biology at Mantis AI, catalyst discovery with guidance from IISc, and applied machine learning at MSU Baroda. I learn by building, and share what I learn through student research and mentorship.

Profile

I’m curious about the space where machines, biology, and human needs meet. I aspire to be a polymath: someone who keeps learning across fields and turns those connections into things that work.

I

The ₹500 beginning

My journey began with a robotics workshop in September 2024. It cost ₹500. Until then, programming mostly lived on a screen; suddenly, software could sense and respond to the physical world. That small opportunity led to prototypes, competitions, research, and a different way of thinking about education.

In January 2025, my team won first prize in the Senior Category of Junior Make-a-Thon at IIT Madras, building a working prototype in under eight hours. I started to see engineering as something I could contribute to right now.

II

Building for the world around me

EutroBot brings together robotics and microbiology to explore water restoration. Four prototypes later, the question still drives the work: how can we treat contamination beneath the surface?

My other projects explore voice biomarkers, embedded machine learning, and recycling incentives. VAANI 2.0 received second prize at LingHacks VII. Each project is a way to learn by solving a specific problem.

III

Research across disciplines

At Mantis AI, I built a reasoning and planning layer for an autonomous computational biology workflow. Under Prof. Chinmoy Ranjan’s guidance at IISc, I work on machine learning for catalyst discovery. With Dr. Neetu Sharma at MSU Baroda, I explore applications in networks, healthcare, and transportation.

My earlier research includes sustainable bioremediation through the Climate Leaders Fellowship, rail innovation through the Lemelson-MIT Program, and solar energy research with an international Junior Academy team. Read about the research →

My work on EutroBot and VAANI 2.0 is available on Figshare. Research abstracts have been accepted for poster presentations at ICWS, VENICE, INBC, and the World Conference on Neurology. In September 2026, I secured All India Rank 33 and Band A Honour in the IISc Young Scientist Challenge.

IV

Making room for other builders

As IT & Innovation Student President at NISV, I lead technical initiatives and share STEM opportunities with 800+ students. At The Project Nirvana, I lead software, hardware, electronics, and web development while mentoring more than 100 students.

I have also mentored through the National Student Research Institution, led my school’s Robotics Club, supported cancer research awareness, and contributed to the inclusive community initiative Dil Se. More on service and mentorship →
