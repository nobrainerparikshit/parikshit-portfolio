Figshare · 19 September 2026

VAANI 2.0: An AI-Powered Vocal Biomarker Framework for Preliminary Parkinson’s Disease Risk Screening

A proof-of-concept framework combining browser-based voice recording, Librosa acoustic feature extraction, and Random Forest classification. Forty MFCC means and forty MFCC standard deviations form an 80-dimensional feature representation. The study evaluates preliminary Parkinson’s disease risk screening from short voice recordings.

In the reported evaluation of 73 recordings, with 15 reserved for testing, accuracy was 80.0%, precision 80.0%, sensitivity 66.7%, F1-score 72.7%, and ROC–AUC 88.9%. The small dataset and test cohort limit generalisation. The work is positioned as preliminary screening and decision support, rather than diagnosis.

Figshare · 10 September 2026

Eutrobot: An Autonomous Unmanned Surface Vehicle Integrating Real Time Environmental Sensing, AI Assisted Algal Detection, and Phytoremediation for Scalable Freshwater Purification

An autonomous surface vehicle combining environmental sensing, GPS-guided navigation, AI-assisted algal detection, and adaptive phytoremediation. The study describes monitoring 17 water-quality parameters, an onboard web server, a Lemna minor biomass deployment algorithm, and a HuskyLens-based algal detection and harvesting system.

Reported field trials at Atladara Lake and Kalali Lake in Vadodara examined navigation and contamination detection. The paper presents an integrated approach to monitoring and freshwater restoration.
